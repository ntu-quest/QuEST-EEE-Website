Part of the global Qiskit Fall Fest, Quantum Fall Fest Singapore 2025 ran across five days and
two campuses. It moved from foundational mathematics to circuits, algorithms, cryptography and
quantum machine learning, with hands-on work throughout.

The festival convened speakers from academia and industry, ran a panel discussion on quantum
advantage and career pathways, and included a visit to the **Centre for Quantum Technologies**.
It brought together students from across Singapore's universities along with participants from
polytechnics and junior colleges — the beginning of the cross-institution network the club works
in now.
