The first session in the **Quantum Questions** series, run with the Singapore Youth Quantum
Collective. Prof. Eugene Chua Yew Siang spoke for an hour on the quantum measurement problem,
and the discussion that followed ran longer than the slot allowed for it — which is the outcome
the series was designed for.

Twenty participants attended alongside seven organisers. Several joined SQYC or the QuEST member
group afterwards.

**What we would change.** Publicity earlier, and a smaller room — a tutorial room rather than a
lecture theatre would have pulled more people into the discussion.
