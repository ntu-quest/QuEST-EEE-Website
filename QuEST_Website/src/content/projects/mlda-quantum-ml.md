## The problem

Quantum machine learning is where most students first want to work and where the entry barrier is
highest: it needs linear algebra, quantum mechanics and a machine-learning background at the same
time. Very few undergraduates arrive with all three.

## The approach

Run with the **Machine Learning & Data Analytics club at EEE (MLDA@EEE)** as a year-long
training-to-project initiative. Students are onboarded through structured workshops and curated
resources, then paired with mentorship to build the technical depth the project needs.

The objective is end-to-end execution of a research-grade project by year end — problem
formulation, literature grounding, implementation, evaluation, and professional reporting.

## What a new member does

Start in the workshops. No prior quantum knowledge is assumed, and the programme is built on the
expectation that you do not have it.

## The two teams

Two teams of six students ran to completion:

- **QAOA for Financial Portfolio Optimization** — the quantum approximate optimisation algorithm
  applied to portfolio selection.
- **Practical Quantum K-Means Clustering** — a working quantum k-means implementation, evaluated
  on real data.
